वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभः ।
निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा ॥

O Lord Ganesha of the curved trunk and mighty form, radiant as a million suns —
remove all obstacles from every endeavour, always.

विघ्न हरण मंगल करण, श्री गणपति महाराज ।
प्रथम निमंत्रण आपको, पूरण कीजो काज ॥

Remover of obstacles, bringer of all that is auspicious —
the first invitation is yours, O Ganpati; bless this occasion to fulfilment.
